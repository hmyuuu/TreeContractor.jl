## Status Definitions
- **[Pending]**: Awaiting assignment.
- **[In Progress]**: Currently being executed by an agent.
- **[Review]**: Completed, waiting for PI/User verification.
- **[Completed]**: Verified and closed.
- **[Paused]**: Suspended for higher priority tasks.
- **[Blocked]**: Waiting for external dependency (see Escalation).

## Agent Roles
- **PI**: Manager, Strategy, QA.
- **PhD-Theory**: Graph Theory, Logic, Proofs.
- **PhD-Algo**: Algorithms, Complexity, Julia Implementation.
- **PhD-Physics**: Domain specific applications (e.g., Quantum).
- **The Writer**: Documentation, Reporting, Visualization.
