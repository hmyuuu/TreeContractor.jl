# Tensor Network Ranks (Ye & Lim, 2019)

**Citation**: Ye, K., & Lim, L. H. (2019). *Tensor network ranks*. arXiv preprint arXiv:1801.02662.
**Status**: [Deep Research in Progress]
**Tags**: #tensor-networks #rank-width #math-physics #G-rank

## 1. Paper Overview
**Core Result**: Generalizes the notion of "Rank" to any underlying graph $G$.
*   Matrix Rank $\leftrightarrow$ Graph $G$ is a single edge.
*   Tensor Rank $\leftrightarrow$ Graph $G$ is a star (CP decomposition).
*   **TT-Rank** (Tensor Train) $\leftrightarrow$ Graph $G$ is a path.
*   **MPS-Rank** $\leftrightarrow$ Graph $G$ is a cycle.
*   **PEPS-Rank** $\leftrightarrow$ Graph $G$ is a 2D Grid.
*   **TTNS-Rank** $\leftrightarrow$ Graph $G$ is a Tree.

**Key Insight**: A tensor might have high rank in one geometry (e.g., Matrix Rank) but low rank in another (e.g., MPS-Rank).
**Theorem**: For any graph $G$, there is a well-defined "G-rank".
**Connection to Rank-Width**: Rank-Width essentially tries to find the *best* Tree $T$ such that the $T$-rank (TTNS-rank) is minimized.
