# Analysis: Rank-width and vertex-minors

**Paper ID:** [Oum 2005]
**Authors:** Sang-il Oum
**Year:** 2005
**Journal:** Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series B

## Theoretical Essence

### Vertex-Minors
The paper introduces the concept of **vertex-minors** to provide a structural characterization of rank-width, analogous to how graph minors characterize treewidth.

*   **Local Complementation ($G * v$):** The operation of replacing the subgraph induced by the neighbors of $v$ with its complement.
    *   *Quantum Connection:* This is equivalent to applying a local Clifford operation (specifically $\sqrt{-iX}$ or similar) on a Graph State.
*   **Vertex-Minor:** A graph $H$ is a vertex-minor of $G$ if $H$ can be obtained from $G$ by a sequence of local complementations and vertex deletions.
*   **Theorem:** Rank-width is monotonic under taking vertex-minors. If $rw(G) \le k$, then $rw(H) \le k$ for all vertex-minors $H$ of $G$.

### Pivoting
*   **Pivot ($G \wedge uv$):** For an edge $uv$, pivoting is defined as applying local complementation at $u$, then $v$, then $u$.
*   **Effect:** This swaps the vertices $u$ and $v$ in the "basis" of the adjacency matrix over $GF(2)$.
*   **Pivot-Minor:** A graph obtained by pivoting and vertex deletions.

## Relevance to Tensor Networks & QC

### The "Hidden" Structure
This paper provides the "missing link" between Graph Theory and Stabilizer Quantum Mechanics.
*   **Graph States:** A graph state $|G\rangle$ is a stabilizer state defined by the adjacency matrix of $G$.
*   **Clifford Group:** The group of operations that map Pauli strings to Pauli strings.
*   **Isomorphism:** Two graph states are **LU-equivalent** (Local Unitary) iff their graphs are related by a sequence of local complementations.
*   **Conclusion:** **Rank-Width measures the complexity of a Quantum State modulo Local Clifford operations.**
    *   Treewidth measures complexity based on *topology* (edges).
    *   Rank-width measures complexity based on *information* (entanglement), invariant under basis changes (pivoting).

## Critical Insight
The "Field Gap" we worried about (GF(2) vs Complex) is actually a feature, not a bug, for **Stabilizer-based** methods. For general tensor networks, rank-width captures the "stabilizer skeleton" of the tensor.
