# Bibliography

## 1. Seminal Theory (Rank-Width, Vertex-Minors)

**[Oum 2005]** Sang-il Oum. "Rank-width and vertex-minors". *Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series B*, 95(1):79–100, 2005.
- **Tags**: `[Theory]`, `[Vertex-Minor]`, `[Essence]`
- **Summary**: Introduces vertex-minors and proves rank-width is monotonic under them. Key link to Local Complementation.

**[Oum 2006]** Sang-il Oum and Paul Seymour. "Approximating clique-width and branch-width". *Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series B*, 96(4):514–528, 2006.
- **Tags**: `[Theory]`, `[Seminal]`
- **Summary**: Defined rank-width to approximate clique-width. First $O(n^3)$ approximation algorithm.

**[Oum 2017]** Sang-il Oum. "Rank-width: Algorithmic and structural results". *Discrete Applied Mathematics*, 2017.
- **Tags**: `[Survey]`, `[Theory]`
- **Summary**: Comprehensive survey of structural results.

**[Oum 2009]** Sang-il Oum. "Computing rank-width exactly". *Information Processing Letters*, 2009.
- **Tags**: `[Algorithm]`, `[Exact]`
- **Summary**: Exponential time exact algorithm $O(2^n)$.

**[Courcelle 2000]** Bruno Courcelle et al. "Linear time solvable optimization problems on graphs of bounded clique-width". *Theory of Computing Systems*, 2000.
- **Tags**: `[Logic]`, `[MSO]`

**[Courcelle 2007]** Bruno Courcelle and Mamadou Moustapha Kanté. "Graph Operations Characterizing Rank-Width and Balanced Graph Expressions". *WG 2007*.
- **Tags**: `[Theory]`, `[Algebraic]`

## 2. Algorithms (Computing Rank-Width)

**[Korhonen 2024]** Tuukka Korhonen and Marek Sokołowski. "Almost-linear time parameterized algorithm for rankwidth via dynamic rankwidth". *arXiv:2402.12364*, 2024.
- **Tags**: `[Algorithm]`, `[State-of-the-Art]`, `[Fast]`
- **Summary**: $O_k(n^{1+o(1)})$ algorithm. Breakthrough for practical implementation.

**[Fomin 2021]** Fedor V. Fomin and Tuukka Korhonen. "Fast FPT-Approximation of Branchwidth". *arXiv*, 2021.
- **Tags**: `[Algorithm]`, `[Branch-width]`

**[Beyß 2013]** Martin Beyß et al. "Fast Algorithm for Rank-Width". *SEA 2013*.
- **Tags**: `[Heuristic]`, `[Algorithm]`

**[Langer 2011]** Alexander Langer et al. "Linear-Time Algorithms for Graphs of Bounded Rankwidth". *Algorithmica*, 2011.
- **Tags**: `[Algorithm]`, `[Game Theory]`

**[Nouwt 2023]** Florian Nouwt and Hans L. Bodlaender. "A simulated annealing method for computing rank-width".
- **Tags**: `[Heuristic]`, `[Practical]`
